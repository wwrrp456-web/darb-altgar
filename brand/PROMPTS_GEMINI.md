## سطر كل مدينة (يُلصق بعد كلمة CITY)
- **riyadh الرياض:** Riyadh, old Najdi city on a plateau: mud-brick fortress with crenelated towers and thick walls (Masmak-style) as the landmark, date palms, sandy streets with baskets of dates and sacks of wheat, wooden doors with geometric carvings, a few men in white thobes and ghutra.
- **jeddah جدة:** Jeddah old town on the Red Sea: tall coral-stone houses with carved wooden roshan balconies, wooden dhow boats and calm turquoise sea on the left side, a small quay with sacks of coffee and baskets of pearls, merchants in light thobes and turbans.
- **hail حائل:** Hail, northern Arabian oasis town beneath red granite Aja mountains: a hilltop fort, green wheat fields and palm groves, a Bedouin black goat-hair tent with a brass coffee pot (dallah) and a couple of camels on the edge, men in brown cloaks.
- **jazan جازان:** Jazan, green southern coastal town: houses of white coral stone with colorful wooden shutters, steep terraced coffee and banana fields on mountains behind, bundles of incense and sacks of coffee beans, fishing boats, soft humid hazy light.
- **cairo القاهرة:** Medieval Cairo: many minarets and domes, mashrabiya wooden balconies, a Nile felucca with a white sail in the right foreground, the Giza pyramids small and hazy on the horizon at sunset, stalls of cotton, papyrus and glass bottles, people in long galabiyas.
- **alex الإسكندرية:** Alexandria Mediterranean port: white-and-sandstone seafront, the ancient Pharos lighthouse tower on a peninsula as the landmark, sailing ships in a blue harbor, quay markets with glass jars and cotton bales, men in light tunics and turbans.
- **aswan أسوان:** Aswan on the Nile: round granite boulders and small islands in the river, white feluccas, colorful Nubian mud-brick houses painted in blue and ochre, palm groves, a desert mountain behind, calm warm light, people in white galabiyas.
- **isfahan أصفهان:** Isfahan, Naqsh-e Jahan style square: a large blue-tiled mosque dome and a turquoise tiled iwan portal as the landmark, long two-level brick arcades, a rectangular pool and fountain in the middle, carpets and brass trays at the stalls, men in long coats and felt hats.
- **shiraz شيراز:** Shiraz garden city: tall dark cypress trees, rose gardens, a pink-tiled mosque with stained glass, a poet's pavilion with a reflecting pool, Zagros mountains behind, people in long colorful coats and caps, turquoise stones and wool at the stalls.
- **tabriz تبريز:** Tabriz bazaar city: a huge brick vaulted covered bazaar entrance as the landmark, snow-capped mountain behind, stacks of woven wool carpets and iron tools, cool bluish morning light with warm lamp glow inside the arcade, people in thick coats and hats.
- **delhi دلهي:** Delhi Mughal city: a red sandstone fort gate with ornate arches and a white marble domed mausoleum behind it, colorful spice market with cones of red, yellow and orange spices and marigold garlands in front, an elephant walking on the road, people in kurtas and turbans, golden dusty light.
- **mumbai بومباي:** Mumbai Arabian Sea harbor: wooden sailing ships and dhows at the waterfront, a stone gateway arch by the water, stacked cotton bales and gem merchants' stalls with small boxes of colorful stones, palm trees, warm humid light, people in cotton kurtas and turbans.
- **kolkata كلكتا:** Kolkata riverside on a wide brown river: stone ghat steps going down to the water, wooden boats loaded with tea chests, colonial-style arched buildings with green shutters, lush trees, misty golden morning light, people in dhotis and shawls.
- **xian شيآن:** Xi'an ancient capital: a massive city wall with a grand gate tower and the tall pagoda behind, a silk caravan arriving through the gate with loaded camels, red lanterns with no writing, stalls of silk bolts and tea boxes, warm dusk light, people in silk robes.
- **guangzhou كانتون:** Guangzhou southern river port: wooden Chinese junks with batten sails on a river, porcelain vases and tea boxes being loaded onto boats, two-story wooden shophouses with curved tiled roofs, humid green hills behind, red lanterns with no writing, people in cotton tunics and conical hats.
- **shanghai شنغهاي:** Shanghai eastern river-and-canal port: many large junks on a wide river, willow trees, arched stone bridges over canals, waterfront warehouses with curved roofs, silk bales and porcelain on the quay, soft hazy sunrise light, people in long robes.
